Review status

What has—and has not—been reviewed.

Automated validation and authoritative source checks support publication, but they are not substitutes for independent human expertise.

Current status: No independent art historian, world historian, accessibility specialist, or image-rights attorney has formally signed off on the complete project.

Requested independent reviews

  1. Art history: periodization, attribution, formal analysis, and historiography.
  2. World history: global framing, chronology, colonial relationships, and omitted traditions.
  3. Accessibility: keyboard, screen reader, magnification, contrast, motion, and cognitive load.
  4. Image rights: license compatibility, attribution, contemporary copyright, and institutional terms.

How review should be recorded

Open a GitHub issue naming the reviewer’s scope, credentials or relevant experience, date, pages examined, findings, and requested revisions. Approval must never be inferred from silence or from checking only one page.
