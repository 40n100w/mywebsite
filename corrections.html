Corrections

History improves through revision.

Factual, attribution, rights, geographic, translation, and accessibility corrections are welcome.

How to report an issue

Use the repository’s Issues tab on GitHub. The static website intentionally contains no form, analytics, or contact tracker.

Please include:

  1. The page and section where the issue appears.
  2. The exact statement, image, map region, or interaction involved.
  3. The proposed correction.
  4. An authoritative museum, archive, publisher, academic, or rights source.
  5. Whether the issue concerns factual accuracy, interpretation, accessibility, privacy, or copyright.

Editorial policy

Corrections should preserve the distinction between established fact and interpretation. Significant revisions should be documented in version history. Rights complaints should be treated promptly; disputed media should be removed while its status is reviewed.
