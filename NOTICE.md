The visual experiments are original teaching diagrams, not reproductions or reconstructions of historical artworks. Chapter designs interpret selected traditions; no single layout represents every culture or movement within an era.

The transparent images in `assets/architecture/` are AI-generated architectural interpretations made for the site, encoded as WebP for delivery. They suggest selected structures and materials across the twelve chapters, including cave mouths, Egyptian columns, Gothic portals, Renaissance arches, Baroque surrounds, Neoclassical porticoes, Romantic ruins, working iron structures, painted garden arbors, and modern exhibition spaces. They are imagined settings, not photographs or reconstructions of actual historical buildings. Their generation descriptions and intended visual references are recorded in `assets/architecture/README.md`.
