Visual language

Words for looking closely.

These terms describe ways images produce meaning. Definitions are intentionally concise and should be read in the context of each era.

Abstraction
Art that emphasizes form, color, line, material, or concept rather than recognizable representation.
Chiaroscuro
Strong modeling through contrasts of light and dark.
Contrapposto
A standing pose in which weight rests primarily on one leg, creating an asymmetrical body.
Humanism
Study centered on human language, agency, history, and classical learning.
Idealization
Changing observed features to embody a preferred standard or concept.
Iconography
The subjects, symbols, and conventional meanings within images.
Linear perspective
A geometric system for representing depth through converging parallel lines.
Naturalism
Representation that closely observes the appearance and behavior of the visible world.
Patronage
Financial or institutional support that enables art to be produced.
Sfumato
Soft transitions between tones that create atmospheric depth and uncertain edges.
The sublime
An experience of awe, vastness, danger, or power that exceeds ordinary comprehension.
Tenebrism
Dramatic illumination in which figures emerge from dominant darkness.
